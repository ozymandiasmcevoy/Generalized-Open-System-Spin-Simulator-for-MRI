Generalized Open-System Spin Simulator for MRI

Interactive density-matrix simulations of MRI spin dynamics using Liouville–von Neumann and Lindblad master equations.

Interactive Simulations

1. Unitary Zeeman Precession Nonrelaxing spin-1/2 evolution under the Zeeman Hamiltonian. 2. Single-Pulse T2 Dephasing Transverse dephasing following RF excitation using Lindblad dynamics. 3. Repeated-Pulse T1 Steady State Repeated excitation and longitudinal recovery toward a steady state. 4. Wigner and Husimi-Q Phase Space Spin phase-space representations of the open-system dynamics.
